======================================================================
WHEELYX - INTEGRATED VEHICLE RENTAL AND FLEET MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
Project Role & Responsibility Matrix
======================================================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Title : Wheelyx — Integrated Vehicle Rental and Fleet Management Platform
Platform Type : Full-Stack Enterprise Web Application (MVC, Servlets, JDBC, MySQL)
Architecture  : Three Role Segregation (Customer, Vehicle Owner, Platform Admin)
Timeline      : 10 Weeks (06 July 2026 - 13 September 2026)

2. TEAM ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Role 1: Team Lead & Full-Stack Architect
- Architecture design, Jakarta EE 10 / Tomcat 10.1 setup, Maven lifecycle.
- Security filters (CustomerAuthFilter, OwnerAuthFilter, AdminAuthFilter).
- Core concurrency-safe booking engine and database transaction integrity.
- Deployment automation, Docker configuration, and Cloudflare Tunnel sharing.

Role 2: Backend & Database Specialist
- Relational schema design (17 tables in 3NF), composite indexes, and seed data.
- HikariCP connection pooling, JDBC DAOs, and parameterized PreparedStatements.
- Billing calculations, 18% GST engine, and simulated payment gateway.
- Fleet maintenance grounding and administrative audit log recording.

Role 3: Frontend & UI/UX Specialist
- Responsive Bootstrap 5.3 UI, custom CSS variables, and persistent Dark/Light theme.
- Dynamic AJAX city/place lookup (/api/places) and vehicle catalog filtering.
- GPS radar telemetry view, Leaflet/Google Maps integration, and Chart.js reporting.
- Printable tax invoices (WX-INV-XXXXXXXX) and customer review submission flows.

Role 4: QA Engineer & Documentation Specialist
- JUnit 5 automated test suite (BillingCalculationTest, ValidationUtilTest, etc.).
- Cross-browser compatibility and mobile responsiveness verification.
- Software Requirements Specification (SRS), UML system models, and weekly reports.
- Slide deck (PPT) authoring, demonstration video walkthrough, and Viva defense prep.

3. FACULTY GUIDE & ACADEMIC SUPERVISION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Periodic weekly milestone review and sign-off.
- Verification of IEEE 830 SRS, UML models, and test reports.
- Final academic evaluation and viva voce assessment.
